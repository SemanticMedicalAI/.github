# SemanticMedicalAI

Research organization focused on semantic medical NLP, ontology generation, graph reasoning and AI-assisted clinical reasoning systems.

---

# Vision

SemanticMedicalAI aims to build modular and scientifically grounded frameworks for transforming unstructured clinical data into semantically interpretable knowledge structures suitable for:

- clinical NLP
- ontology generation
- semantic reasoning
- graph-based AI systems
- explainable medical AI
- LLM-supported clinical assistance

The long-term goal is the development of transparent and semantically structured medical AI pipelines capable of supporting interpretable and evidence-linked clinical reasoning.

---

# Research Focus

The organization currently focuses on the following research areas:

- Semantic Medical NLP
- Knowledge Graph Construction
- Ontology Engineering
- Clinical Concept Extraction
- Graph-Based Reasoning
- Explainable AI
- Medical Information Structuring
- LLM-Supported Clinical Systems
- Neuro-symbolic AI Architectures
